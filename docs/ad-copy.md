# Ad copy — Hinglish

Paste into Ads Manager beside each video. Meta shows ~125 characters of primary
text before "…more", so the first line carries the message on its own.

Destination for every ad: the landing page, with UTMs (see `campaign-plan.md`).
CTA button: **Learn More** (it is a landing page, not a direct install).

Rules every line below already follows (`compliance-checklist.md`): no profit,
no win rate, no "safe"/"guaranteed", no exchange logo, no competitor named.

---

## Ad 01 — Signal in seconds (`out/01-signal-in-seconds.mp4`)

> Signal aaya jab move ho chuka? Lumin ka engine 24/7 scan karta hai — setup bante hi signal, seconds mein. Auto Trade ON ho to entry, stop-loss aur target aapke Binance account pe khud lag jaate hain. Signals FREE.

> Top pe entry = trap. Lumin setup pe signal deta hai, move ke baad nahi. Entry, stop, target — sab clear. Signals free, Auto Trade optional.
